          How to read this screen
          

            

              
Entry / desired price

              

                The decision is made on today's close, but the fill is the
                next open. There is no fixed limit price in the
                strategy. The order card shows today's close as a reference;
                enter the actual open there to recalculate the stop and size.
              

            

            

              
Stop loss (SL)

              

                7.5% below the actual fill. The table and order
                cards show a reference stop based on today's close. Recalculate
                it from the real next-open fill before placing an order.
              

            

            

              
Take profit (TP)

              

                There is no fixed take-profit. The strategy
                exits on its structural exit (the 10-day close low for Donchian), the
                7.5% stop, or after 20 sessions. The order card shows the
                current exit trigger.
              

            

            

              
Fees

              

                Sizing reserves the round-trip cost. The
                default EGX model is 2 EGP + 0.10% per executed order,
                per side (research baseline: 0.30%/side + 0 fixed).
                Cards show the round-trip fee, its share of the risk budget,
                and the break-even move. A highlighted fee drag means the win
                can be largely eaten by costs.
              

            

            

              
Checklist gates

              

                H = history ≥ 60 bars · F = fresh session · V = volume > 0 ·
                P = close ≥ 1 EGP · L = 20-day median traded value ≥ 2,000,000
                EGP. Hover a chip or open the row for the actual value.
